# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

**Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics Task 1 (2026)**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a business sales performance analysis of a fictional US-based superstore using four years of transactional data (2014–2017). The goal was to analyse revenue trends, product performance, regional profitability, and the impact of discounting — and to present findings in a client-ready Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

- Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
- How has sales performance changed over time?
- Which regions are most and least profitable?
- Are discounts hurting the business's bottom line?
- Where should the business focus to grow faster?
